<div class="welcome-box">
<p>I am an applied mathematician working in epidemiology, with a focus on data-driven modeling of infectious diseases. My research centers on real-time analysis of emerging outbreaks, transmission dynamics, and statistical inference for both infectious and non-communicable diseases. I am also interested in the interplay between mathematical modeling and public health policy.</p>

<p>I am an Associate Professor in the <a href="https://ghp.ntu.edu.tw/">Global Health Program</a> and the <a href="https://epm.ntu.edu.tw/web/index/index.jsp">Institute of Epidemiology and Preventive Medicine</a>, College of Public Health, National Taiwan University, Taipei, Taiwan, and Associate Director of the Global Health Program. Since 2020 I have worked closely with the Epidemic Intelligence Center of Taiwan Centers for Disease Control. Our <a href="/my-group/">group</a> analyzes outbreaks as they unfold; see <a href="/research-interests/">Research</a> for current projects and <a href="/opportunities/">Opportunities</a> if you would like to join us.</p>

We welcome NTU students who want to learn how infectious disease outbreaks are analyzed with quantitative methods. Everyone in the group works on real outbreak data from the start, usually on questions that come directly from public health agencies. Group meetings are in English, and members come from the Global Health Program, the Institute of Epidemiology and Preventive Medicine, the Institute of Health Data Analytics and Statistics and the Department of Public Health.

We do not have funded positions for postdoctoral researchers or external research assistants, and we do not run an open program for visiting researchers. Visits are arranged directly with colleagues we already work with (see below).

## Graduate students at NTU

Master's and doctoral students join through the [Global Health Program](https://ghp.ntu.edu.tw/) (taught in English; see the [MSc](https://ghp.ntu.edu.tw/course/master) and [PhD](https://ghp.ntu.edu.tw/course/phd) pages and the [application instructions](https://ghp.ntu.edu.tw/credit/master_degree)), the [Institute of Epidemiology and Preventive Medicine](https://epm.ntu.edu.tw/web/index/index.jsp) or the Institute of Health Data Analytics and Statistics. Students admitted to one of these programs can choose me as thesis advisor or ask to be co-advised; students from other institutes of the College are welcome to attend group meetings before deciding.

No prior training in mathematics or programming is required, but curiosity about data and a willingness to learn R, Python or Stan are essential. Typical thesis projects estimate transmission parameters for an ongoing outbreak, evaluate the effect of interventions, or build forecasts for a disease of public health concern in Taiwan or the student's home country.

If you are an NTU student and would like to work with us, send an email to **akhmetzhanov [at] ntu.edu.tw** with your program and year and a short description of the public health problem you would like to work on, or simply come to one of the group meetings (Thursdays at 12:30, Room 545, during the semester). Prospective applicants to the Global Health Program are welcome to get in touch before applying; the program's pages list deadlines, and the [NTU Office of International Affairs](https://oia.ntu.edu.tw/en) lists the scholarships available to international students.

## NTU undergraduates

Undergraduate students of the College of Public Health can join the group through NSTC College Student Research Projects or by sitting in on group meetings. Several undergraduate projects have led to conference presentations and peer-reviewed papers.

## Visiting colleagues

Visits by collaborators and their students are arranged directly with me, for instance within our exchanges with Kyoto University and other partner groups; the group also takes part in the Kyoto University summer boot camp on infectious disease modeling and in summer schools at NTU. If we already work together and you would like to spend time in Taipei, write to me well in advance so that hosting arrangements can be made.

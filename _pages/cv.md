## Teaching

**National Taiwan University, College of Public Health**

- Principles of Biostatistics (required course, Global Health Program)
- Applied Quantitative Methods in Global Health (required course for doctoral students, Global Health Program)
- COVID-19 Pandemic: Science, Response and Future Prospects (elective, co-developed with Prof. Hsien-Ho Lin)
- Public Health (undergraduate course, co-instructor)
- Study Design of Epidemiology and the master's and doctoral seminar of the Institute of Epidemiology and Preventive Medicine (seminar chair, 2022–2025)
- Practical Guide for Analysis of Infectious Disease Outbreaks (intensive winter workshop for NTU students and Taiwan CDC staff, 2021–2024)
- NTU summer school on infectious disease modeling: nowcasting, forecasting and ensemble models (2025)

**Elsewhere**

- Kyoto University Summer Boot Camp on infectious disease modeling: calculus and linear algebra refreshers, stability analysis of epidemic models, epidemiological time intervals (2024–2026)
- Introduction to Modelling, McMaster University (2012)
- Calculus I, Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (2008–2009)

Students rated the elective courses between 4.56 and 5.00 out of 5. Recipient of the 2023 NTU Excellent Teaching Award for English-taught courses.
